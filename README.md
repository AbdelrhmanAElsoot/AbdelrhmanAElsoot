### 👨‍💻 About Me
I bridge the gap between **Naval Architecture** and **Artificial Intelligence**. My work focuses on leveraging first-principles engineering and deep learning to solve complex hydrodynamic and structural challenges.

- 🎓 Senior Undergraduate in **Naval Architecture & Marine Engineering** at Alexandria University.
- 🧠 Currently researching **Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)** & **Operators (PINO)** for hydrodynamic parameter identification and ship design optimization.
- 🚢 Building an automated Decision Support System (DSS) for optimal cargo stowage and ballast allocation under IMO 2008 IS Code.
- 💻 Passionate about automating engineering workflows across CFD simulations, numerical algorithms, and data pipelines.
